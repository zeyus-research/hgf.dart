## Against the Linux machine

Linux: Intel i7-7700 (4 cores, 8 threads, 2017) with an AMD RX 7600 XT
(RADV, wgpu-native); macOS: Apple M3 Pro (6 performance + 6 efficiency
cores), its GPU through Metal (wgpu-native). Same Dart (3.13.7) and pyhgf
commit. Milliseconds unless noted; ratio = Linux / macOS (above 1: the Mac
is faster).

The order is the same on both machines: Dart AOT ahead of JAX and rshgf on
single series (1.7× JAX f64 on the Mac, 1.4× on Linux). On the batch, a
core of the M3 runs `BatchNetwork` 2.3× faster, and 12 isolates beat JAX's
float32 `vmap` 2.5× (Linux, 8 isolates: 0.55×). JAX's `vmap` hardly
changes between the machines (the XLA CPU backend vectorises with AVX2 on
the i7 and NEON on the M3). The GPU kernel takes the same time on both
GPUs: at 1000 × 1000 it is latency-bound.

Per learning step (Adam). CPU: hgf_deep f64 on one core.

A single `batchUpdate` with its readback costs ≥ 1.24 ms on the Mac
(1.1–1.3 ms on Linux for the small and medium networks).

Sequential fits (`GpuDeepEnsemble`), µs per member-sample at saturation,
and one network on one CPU core:

| network | CPU one network, Linux | macOS | GPU saturated, Linux | macOS |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| small | 10 | 7.1 | 0.12 | 0.082 |
| medium | 132 | 77.7 | 0.62 | 1.19 |
| wide | 1280 | 632 | 14 | 20.3 |

The M3's GPU is faster on the small, barrier-bound networks and slower once
the weights and Adam moments stream through memory (medium, wide); against
its own, faster CPU core the speed-up at saturation is 86× / 65× / 31×
(Linux 84× / 214× / 90×).

### Fitting (`bench:gpu-fit`)

| job | Linux pool (8) | macOS pool (12) | Linux GPU | macOS GPU |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| MAP, 2000 agents | 332 | 116 | 89 | 80 |
| NUTS, 200 agents | 34 000 | 9 120 | 5 000–5 400 | 11 500 |
| NUTS, 2000 agents | 319 000 | 90 900 | 30 000 | 23 400 |

A GPU pass on the Mac waits ~1.4 ms for its results (NUTS at 20 agents:
4615 passes, 6.6 s waiting), against ~0.3–0.5 ms on Linux, so small NUTS
jobs are better on the 12-core pool there (200 agents: 9.1 s on the pool,
11.5 s on the GPU). At 2000 agents the GPU wins on both machines (3.9× on
the Mac).

### Gradients by mode (new)

[DIFFERENTIATION.md](DIFFERENTIATION.md) §7: on the M3's GPU forward-mode
tangents spill sooner than on the RX 7600 XT (8 binary inputs, k = 5: 21–25×
the primal against 7–14×), so reverse mode pays from fewer parameters
there. CPU reverse mode (new) was measured on the Mac only.
